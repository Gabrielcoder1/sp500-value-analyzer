# S&P 500 Value Analyzer

## Project Goal

The goal of this project is to understand what an investor is actually buying when they buy the S&P 500.

This project will analyze the companies inside the S&P 500 by looking at company age, sector distribution, industry distribution, estimated market-cap weights, concentration, and valuation compared to book value.

## Questions This Project Will Answer

- What companies are inside the S&P 500?
- Which sectors and industries dominate the index?
- How old are the companies in the index?
- How concentrated is the index in the largest companies?
- How much of every $100 invested goes to each company or sector?
- What is the estimated total market value compared to combined book value?
- Which sectors appear more expensive based on price-to-book ratios?

## Data Sources

- S&P 500 company list from Wikipedia
- Financial data from yfinance / Yahoo Finance

## Planned Analysis Steps

1. Load the S&P 500 company list
2. Clean company names, tickers, sectors, industries, and dates
3. Calculate company age
4. Pull market cap and valuation data
5. Estimate S&P 500 company weights
6. Analyze sector and industry exposure
7. Compare total market value to estimated book value
8. Create charts and summary tables
9. Write conclusions in simple language
